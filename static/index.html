About MultiMedAI

An educational & research assistant for medical imaging and literature — built on free, open models. Not a medical device; not a diagnosis.

What it does

  • Image search — retrieves real images from ~6,800 open cases (pathology + radiology + chest disease).
  • Image analysis — vision-model narration + trained chest finding classifier (~94%) + explainable reasoning.
  • Region highlight — Grad-CAM attention (indicative only).
  • Document RAG — upload a paper/thesis, get page-cited answers & reports.
  • Knowledge base — answers grounded in 8,192 PubMed passages.
  • Literature — live PubMed + Google Scholar links for evidence.

Models (all free / open)

  • BiomedCLIP — retrieval, modality & finding features
  • Gemini 2.0 Flash (if key set) or Qwen2.5 / moondream local fallback
  • MiniLM — document & KB embeddings · SD-Turbo — synthesis (experimental)

Honest limits

  • Outputs are educational, never diagnostic.
  • Region highlighting is model-attention, not a validated detector.
  • Generated images are synthetic, not real scans.
